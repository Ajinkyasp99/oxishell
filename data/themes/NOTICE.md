# Bundled themes

The `.yaml` files in this directory are copied unmodified from
[warpdotdev/themes](https://github.com/warpdotdev/themes) (directories `warp_bundled/` and `standard/`),
licensed under the Apache License 2.0 — see `LICENSE-warp-themes`. Copyright remains with Warp and the
original theme authors. Many of these palettes are ports of well-known color schemes (Dracula, Nord,
Solarized, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, ...) whose own projects are credited in the upstream repository.

Background images referenced by some Warp themes (`background_image`) are **not** bundled; those themes use
their colors only. Your own themes in `~/.config/oxishell/themes/` may reference images.
